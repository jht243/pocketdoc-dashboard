title: Nutrition genes - lactase, caffeine, FTO and gene-based diets

Nutrition and diet markers are common on consumer reports. A few have clear meaning. The LCT region variant rs4988235 is strongly linked with whether lactase activity persists into adulthood, so it predicts lactose tolerance well, though symptoms still vary. The CYP1A2 variant rs762551 affects how fast caffeine is cleared, and some studies tie slow clearance to more heart risk with heavy coffee intake, but findings are mixed.

The FTO variant rs9939609 is the best-known obesity-associated variant. Each copy is linked with roughly 1 to 1.5 kg higher average body weight, a small effect, and studies show carriers lose weight on lifestyle programs about as well as non-carriers.

The Food4Me trial, run in seven European countries, found that personalized nutrition advice improved diets more than generic advice, but adding genetic information did not make the advice work any better than personalizing it on diet and blood markers alone.

So for nutrition, the member's measured labs and habits are the better guide; genotype can explain a tendency, such as lactose symptoms, but rarely changes the plan.
