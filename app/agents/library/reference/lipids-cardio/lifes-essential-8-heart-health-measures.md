Life's Essential 8 (LE8) is the American Heart Association's definition of cardiovascular health, published as a 2022 presidential advisory in Circulation (Lloyd-Jones et al.). It replaced Life's Simple 7 and is meant to apply across the whole life course, children as well as adults.

The eight measures fall into two groups:

- Health behaviors: diet, physical activity, nicotine exposure, and sleep
- Health factors: body mass index (BMI), blood lipids, blood glucose, and blood pressure

Changes from Life's Simple 7: sleep was added, with 7 to 9 hours a night as the adult goal; nicotine exposure now counts vaping and e-cigarettes as well as secondhand smoke; blood lipids are scored on non-HDL cholesterol rather than total cholesterol, so fasting is not needed; and blood glucose can be scored with hemoglobin A1c as well as glucose.

Each measure is scored from 0 to 100, and the overall score is the unweighted average of the eight. For some purposes the advisory groups overall scores as high (80 or higher), moderate (50 to 79) and low (under 50), while treating the continuous 0 to 100 score as the main measure. The advisory treats social and psychological factors as the foundation that shapes all eight. Keywords: Life's Essential 8, LE8 score, heart health score, AHA cardiovascular health, Life's Simple 7.
