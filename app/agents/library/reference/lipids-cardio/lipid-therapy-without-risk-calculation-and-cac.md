Some groups qualify for cholesterol-lowering therapy under the 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety dyslipidemia guideline without a PREVENT risk calculation:

- LDL-C of 190 mg/dL or higher, or known familial hypercholesterolemia (FH, inherited high cholesterol).
- Adults aged 40 or older with diabetes (type 1 or type 2), chronic kidney disease stage 3 or higher, or HIV, for whom the guideline recommends clinicians initiate lipid-lowering therapy.
- Younger adults with LDL-C of 160 mg/dL or higher, for whom earlier treatment can be considered.

Coronary artery calcium (CAC) scoring is a noncontrast CT scan of the heart arteries. The guideline recommends it selectively for men over 40 and women over 45 whose 10-year risk is borderline or intermediate and the treatment decision is uncertain. Any detectable calcium supports an LDL-C goal under 100 mg/dL, with lower goals at higher calcium scores. The PREVENT risk tiers themselves apply to adults 30 to 79 with LDL-C between 70 and 189 mg/dL. Keywords: calcium score, heart scan, coronary calcium, familial hypercholesterolemia, very high LDL.
