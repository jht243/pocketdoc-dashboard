For secondary prevention, meaning people who already have atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) such as a prior heart attack, stroke, stent or bypass, or peripheral artery disease, the 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety dyslipidemia guideline sets absolute goals:

- Clinical or subclinical ASCVD: LDL-C under 70 mg/dL and non-HDL-C under 100 mg/dL.
- Very high risk ASCVD, for example ASCVD with additional high-risk conditions: LDL-C under 55 mg/dL and non-HDL-C under 85 mg/dL.

The guideline also calls for at least a 50% reduction in LDL-C in most high-risk groups. Summaries of the guideline note that a coronary artery calcium score of 1,000 or more is treated as equivalent to very high risk.

Non-HDL cholesterol is total cholesterol minus HDL cholesterol; it captures all the cholesterol carried in particles that can build plaque, and it is printed on many standard lipid panels.
