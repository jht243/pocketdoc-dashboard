The 2025 AHA/ACC high blood pressure guideline uses the AHA PREVENT equations, for adults 30 to 79, to decide when stage 1 hypertension needs medication:

- BP of 130/80 mm Hg or higher with cardiovascular disease, diabetes, chronic kidney disease, or a 10-year PREVENT-CVD risk of 7.5% or higher: the guideline recommends starting blood pressure medication along with lifestyle changes.
- Stage 1 with PREVENT-CVD risk under 7.5% and none of those conditions: a 3- to 6-month trial of lifestyle changes first, with medication recommended if BP stays at or above 130/80.
- Average BP of 140/90 or higher: medication is recommended along with lifestyle changes.

The general treatment target for most adults is below 130/80 mm Hg, and the guideline encourages a systolic below 120 mm Hg for adults at increased cardiovascular risk when it can be reached safely. Lifestyle measures the guideline emphasizes include a heart-healthy eating pattern, less sodium, more potassium-rich foods unless restricted, regular physical activity, weight management, and limiting alcohol.
