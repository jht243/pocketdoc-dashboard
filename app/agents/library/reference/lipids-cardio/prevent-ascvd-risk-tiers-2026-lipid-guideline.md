The 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety dyslipidemia guideline (JACC and Circulation, March 2026) replaced the 2018 cholesterol guideline and switched risk estimation to the AHA PREVENT-ASCVD equations. For adults aged 30 to 79 without known heart disease (atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, ASCVD), the guideline sorts 10-year PREVENT-ASCVD risk into four tiers:

- Low: under 3%
- Borderline: 3% to under 5%
- Intermediate: 5% to under 10%
- High: 10% or higher

These tiers decide whether cholesterol-lowering therapy is discussed and which LDL cholesterol goal applies. The guideline treats a 10-year risk of 3% or more as the point where clinicians may begin considering lipid-lowering therapy; at borderline risk this is guided by risk-enhancing factors, and at intermediate risk therapy is recommended after a clinician-patient discussion. Low-risk adults are managed with lifestyle.

PREVENT starts a decade earlier than the older Pooled Cohort Equations, which began at age 40, and gives lower risk numbers than the old calculator for most people. A heart risk percentage from the old calculator is not directly comparable to a PREVENT percentage.
