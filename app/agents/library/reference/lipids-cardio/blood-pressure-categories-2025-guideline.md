The 2025 AHA/ACC high blood pressure guideline (published in Circulation and Hypertension, August 2025) kept the adult categories introduced in 2017. Blood pressure (BP) is written systolic over diastolic in mm Hg:

- Normal: systolic under 120 and diastolic under 80
- Elevated: systolic 120 to 129 with diastolic under 80
- Stage 1 hypertension: systolic 130 to 139 or diastolic 80 to 89
- Stage 2 hypertension: systolic 140 or higher or diastolic 90 or higher
- Severe hypertension: above 180 systolic or above 120 diastolic

When systolic and diastolic fall in different categories, the higher category applies. The guideline bases diagnosis on an average of readings, ideally two or more readings taken on two or more separate occasions, and supports confirming with home or ambulatory monitoring. A single high reading at one visit does not by itself establish hypertension. Severe readings with symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, weakness, vision change or confusion are treated as an emergency.
