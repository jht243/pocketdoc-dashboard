The 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety dyslipidemia guideline uses risk-enhancing factors mainly for adults whose 10-year PREVENT-ASCVD risk is borderline (3% to under 5%). When present, they favor starting or intensifying cholesterol-lowering therapy. Clinical summaries of the guideline list:

- Premature ASCVD in a first-degree relative (men under 55, women under 65)
- Higher-risk ancestry, such as South Asian or Filipino
- Chronic inflammatory disease such as rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis or lupus
- Lp(a) of 125 nmol/L (50 mg/dL) or higher
- hs-CRP of 2 mg/L or higher, on more than one measurement, after excluding other causes
- Pregnancy-related history such as gestational diabetes or preeclampsia, or menopause before 40
- Cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) syndrome

Coronary artery calcium scoring can also be used selectively to refine risk when the decision is uncertain. Exact wording of each factor should be checked against the full guideline. Keywords: family history of heart disease, risk enhancers, inflammation and heart risk, early menopause, preeclampsia.
