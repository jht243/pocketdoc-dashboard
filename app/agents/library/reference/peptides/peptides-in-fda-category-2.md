Category 2 is FDA's interim list of bulk substances nominated for compounding that FDA has identified as raising significant safety risks. Compounding with Category 2 substances is not covered by FDA's interim enforcement-discretion policy.

On FDA's page, current as of April 22, 2026, Category 2 still includes several growth hormone secretagogue and related peptides, among them:

- GHRP-2 (injectable and nasal routes)
- GHRP-6
- Kisspeptin-10

The same page lists, separately, a group of peptides whose nominations were withdrawn by their nominators, including BPC-157, TB-500 (thymosin beta-4 fragment), CJC-1295, AOD-9604, Melanotan II, MOTS-c, KPV, Semax, Selank, Epitalon, thymosin alpha-1, LL-37, dihexa and PEG-MGF. Most of these left Category 2 in FDA's April 2026 action. Withdrawal of a nomination does not mean FDA found these substances safe, and it does not put them on the 503A bulks list.

FDA's lists change; agents should give the date of the status they describe. Questions about a specific product belong with the prescribing clinician and dispensing pharmacy.
