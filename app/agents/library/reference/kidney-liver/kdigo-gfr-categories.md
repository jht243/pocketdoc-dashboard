The KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline groups glomerular filtration rate (GFR, eGFR, estimated kidney function, kidney filtration) into six categories, in mL/min/1.73m²:

- G1: 90 or above, normal or high.
- G2: 60 to 89, mildly decreased relative to young-adult levels.
- G3a: 45 to 59, mildly to moderately decreased.
- G3b: 30 to 44, moderately to severely decreased.
- G4: 15 to 29, severely decreased.
- G5: below 15, kidney failure.

KDIGO notes that without evidence of kidney damage, neither G1 nor G2 meets the criteria for CKD; an eGFR in the 60s with a normal urine albumin is not chronic kidney disease by this definition. The guideline also asks labs to flag an adult eGFR below 60 mL/min/1.73m² as low. Risk of kidney progression and cardiovascular events is judged from the GFR category and the albuminuria category together, as shown on the KDIGO heat map, rather than from eGFR alone.
