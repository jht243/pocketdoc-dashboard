For adults with chronic kidney disease (CKD, reduced kidney function, kidney disease) and high blood pressure, the KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline, endorsing the KDIGO 2021 blood pressure guideline, suggests a target systolic blood pressure (SBP, top number) below 120 mm Hg when tolerated (Recommendation 3.4.1, graded 2B).

Key conditions attached to this target:

- It applies to standardized office blood pressure measurement, taken with a protocol after rest. KDIGO warns it may be hazardous to apply the under-120 target to readings taken in a nonstandardized way. Home monitoring is a practical way to identify high blood pressure.
- Less intensive treatment should be considered in people with frailty, high risk of falls and fractures, very limited life expectancy, or symptoms of low blood pressure on standing (postural hypotension).
- The rationale is mainly cardiovascular. Trials have not shown that intensive lowering meaningfully reduces kidney failure, but SPRINT data support cardiovascular benefit, and aiming below 120 helps more people reach at least below 130.

This is consistent with the 2025 AHA/ACC high blood pressure guideline, which sets a general target below 130/80 mm Hg and encourages a systolic below 120 for people at increased cardiovascular risk when it can be reached safely. Any change to blood pressure treatment is made by the treating clinician.
