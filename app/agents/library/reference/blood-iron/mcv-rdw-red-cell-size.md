MCV (mean corpuscular volume, average red blood cell size) and RDW (red cell distribution width, variation in red cell size) are reported on every CBC.

- Microcytosis is an MCV below 80 fL in adults, per the American Family Physician review on microcytosis. The most common causes are iron deficiency and thalassemia trait; anemia of chronic disease, lead toxicity and sideroblastic anemia are other causes. Ferritin is the recommended first test.
- Normocytic is roughly 80 to 100 fL.
- Macrocytosis is an MCV above 100 fL, per the American Family Physician review on macrocytosis. The most common causes are alcohol use, vitamin B12 or folate deficiency, and medications; hypothyroidism, liver disease and hemolysis are others. That review recommends a B12 level for anyone with an elevated MCV.

RDW measures how uneven red cell sizes are. It is often increased in iron deficiency and normal in anemia of chronic disease, but the microcytosis review notes RDW is not sensitive or specific enough on its own to tell iron deficiency from beta-thalassemia trait.
