Weight lost on a GLP-1 medicine such as semaglutide (Wegovy, Ozempic) or tirzepatide (Zepbound, Mounjaro) is not all fat. The Wegovy and Zepbound prescribing information (both revised in 2026) each state that the drug lowers body weight with greater fat mass loss than lean mass loss, which means some lean mass (muscle and other fat-free tissue) is lost as well. How much lean mass is lost, and how best to protect it, is an active research question; protein intake and resistance training are commonly discussed with the care team.

These drugs slow stomach emptying, which can change how other pills are absorbed:
- The Zepbound label warns that tirzepatide may reduce the effectiveness of birth control pills (oral hormonal contraceptives), with the largest effect after the first dose. It advises people using the pill to switch to a non-oral method or add a barrier method for 4 weeks after starting and for 4 weeks after each dose increase.
- Both labels advise caution and closer monitoring for oral medicines where small changes in blood level matter, such as warfarin (narrow therapeutic index drugs).
- Taken with insulin or a sulfonylurea, these drugs raise the risk of low blood sugar.

Birth control, warfarin and diabetes medicines are worth reviewing with the prescriber when a GLP-1 drug is started or its dose is changed.
