Semaglutide (Wegovy, Ozempic, Rybelsus) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist; tirzepatide (Zepbound, Mounjaro) acts on both GIP and GLP-1 receptors. Both are used for weight loss and type 2 diabetes.

Stomach and gut side effects are the most common. The Wegovy prescribing information (revised March 2026) lists nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal pain, indigestion, bloating, burping and reflux among the most common adverse reactions, and warns that GI reactions are sometimes severe; it is not recommended in severe gastroparesis. Vomiting and diarrhea can cause dehydration and acute kidney injury, so the label advises monitoring kidney function when they occur.

Other label warnings and precautions:
- Boxed warning: thyroid C-cell tumors in rodents, with human relevance unknown; contraindicated with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or MEN 2.
- Acute pancreatitis and gallbladder disease (gallstones).
- Low blood sugar when combined with insulin or a sulfonylurea.
- Diabetic retinopathy complications in people with type 2 diabetes, and a higher heart rate.
- Pulmonary aspiration during anesthesia or deep sedation, so planned surgeries and procedures should be disclosed.

Severe or persistent abdominal pain, ongoing vomiting or signs of dehydration are reasons to contact the prescriber promptly.
