The 2009 harmonized definition of metabolic syndrome (insulin resistance syndrome, syndrome X), a joint statement from the International Diabetes Federation, NHLBI, American Heart Association and other societies, requires any three of five findings. No single finding is mandatory.

- Elevated waist circumference, using population- and country-specific cut points. US definitions commonly use 40 inches (102 cm) or more for men and 35 inches (88 cm) or more for women; lower cut points apply to some ancestries, such as Asian populations.
- Triglycerides of 150 mg/dL or higher, or drug treatment for high triglycerides.
- HDL cholesterol ('good' cholesterol) below 40 mg/dL in men or below 50 mg/dL in women, or drug treatment for low HDL.
- Blood pressure of 130/85 mmHg or higher, or treatment for hypertension.
- Fasting glucose of 100 mg/dL or higher, or drug treatment for high blood sugar.

The statement unified earlier, conflicting ATP III and IDF definitions. Metabolic syndrome identifies a cluster of risk factors linked to higher risk of type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease; it is a risk marker rather than a diagnosis of either.
