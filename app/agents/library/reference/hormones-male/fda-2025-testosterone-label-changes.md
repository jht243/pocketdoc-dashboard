On February 28, 2025, the FDA announced class-wide labeling changes for all testosterone products (TRT gels, injections, patches, oral and buccal forms):

- Results of the TRAVERSE trial, which found testosterone noninferior to placebo for heart attack, stroke and cardiovascular death in men with hypogonadism and high cardiovascular risk, are added to all labels.
- Boxed warning language about an increased risk of adverse cardiovascular outcomes is removed from all testosterone products.
- Blood pressure: required ambulatory blood pressure studies showed testosterone products raise blood pressure. Products with completed studies get product-specific blood pressure information, and products without a blood pressure warning get a new one.
- The Limitation of Use for age-related hypogonadism is kept.

That last point reflects how FDA frames approval: FDA states testosterone is approved solely for men who have low testosterone together with an associated medical condition, such as disease of the testes or pituitary. Testosterone labels state that safety and efficacy in men with age-related ("late-onset") hypogonadism, low testosterone due to aging alone, have not been established. Labels also warn that testosterone has been abused at high doses, with serious heart and psychiatric effects.

Removal of the heart boxed warning does not remove other warnings, such as blood clots, high hematocrit, and the gel secondary-exposure boxed warning. Home blood pressure checks after starting are worth discussing with the prescriber.
