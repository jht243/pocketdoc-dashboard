The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) June 2023 recommendation on screening adults for anxiety disorders:

- Adults 19 to 64, including pregnant and postpartum people: screening for anxiety disorders is recommended (grade B).
- Adults 65 and older: evidence is insufficient to weigh the benefits and harms of screening (I statement), so clinicians use judgment for each person.

The recommendation applies to adults without a diagnosed mental health condition and without recognized symptoms of anxiety. Screening tools named include the Generalized Anxiety Disorder scales (GAD-2 and GAD-7), the anxiety subscale of the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale, and the Geriatric Anxiety Scale and Geriatric Anxiety Inventory for older adults. A positive screen is followed by a confirmatory diagnostic assessment.

Evidence on how often to rescreen is limited. The Task Force suggests screening adults who have never been screened, and considering risk factors and life events when deciding whether to repeat it. Keywords: anxiety screening, GAD-7, worry, nervousness, panic, generalized anxiety disorder test.
