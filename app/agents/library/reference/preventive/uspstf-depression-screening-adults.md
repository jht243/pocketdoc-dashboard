The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) June 2023 recommendation covers adults 19 and older, including pregnant and postpartum people and adults 65 and older, who have no diagnosed mental health condition and no recognized signs of depression:

- Screening for major depressive disorder (depression): recommended (grade B), with a moderate net benefit.
- Screening for suicide risk in adults without symptoms: evidence is insufficient to weigh benefits and harms (I statement).

Common screening questionnaires named in the statement include the Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-2 and PHQ-9), the Geriatric Depression Scale for older adults, and the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale for pregnant and postpartum people. A positive screen is followed by a full clinical assessment to confirm a diagnosis and plan care.

The I statement on suicide risk screening does not mean concern should be ignored: anyone with thoughts of self-harm or suicide needs urgent help, and in the US the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline is available by call or text.
