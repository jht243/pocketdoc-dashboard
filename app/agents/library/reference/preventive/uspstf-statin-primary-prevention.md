The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) 2022 recommendation covers adults without known cardiovascular disease:

- Ages 40 to 75 with one or more risk factors (high cholesterol, diabetes, high blood pressure or smoking) and a 10-year cardiovascular risk of 10% or higher: the Task Force recommends clinicians prescribe a statin (grade B).
- Ages 40 to 75 with one or more risk factors and a 10-year risk of 7.5% to under 10%: clinicians may selectively offer a statin, since the expected benefit is smaller (grade C).
- Age 76 and older: evidence is insufficient to judge starting a statin for primary prevention (I statement).

This recommendation used the ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equations to estimate risk, not the newer PREVENT equations, so its percentage cut-offs do not map directly onto PREVENT results or onto the 2026 ACC/AHA cholesterol guideline tiers. It does not apply to adults with LDL cholesterol above 190 mg/dL or known familial hypercholesterolemia, who are covered by other guidelines.
