The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) January 2021 recommendation on quitting smoking:

- Nonpregnant adults: clinicians should ask all adults about tobacco use, advise those who use tobacco to stop, and provide behavioral support plus FDA-approved medication (grade A). The FDA-approved options named are nicotine replacement therapy, bupropion sustained-release, and varenicline.
- Pregnant people: ask, advise to stop, and provide behavioral support (grade A). Evidence on cessation medication in pregnancy is insufficient (I statement).
- E-cigarettes for quitting, in all adults including pregnant people: evidence is insufficient to judge benefits and harms (I statement). The Task Force advises directing people to cessation methods with proven effectiveness and established safety instead.

The statement notes that combining behavioral support with medication raises quit rates compared with usual care or medication alone. Free quit support is available in the US at 1-800-QUIT-NOW. Smoking cessation is also part of nicotine exposure in the AHA Life's Essential 8 heart health score. Keywords: quit smoking, stop smoking, nicotine patch, vaping to quit, smoking cessation help.
