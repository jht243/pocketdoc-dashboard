The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) 2021 recommendation (grade A) is to screen all adults 18 and older without known hypertension for high blood pressure, starting with an office blood pressure measurement.

Before treatment begins, the Task Force recommends confirming the diagnosis with readings taken outside the clinic, using either home blood pressure monitoring or 24-hour ambulatory monitoring. This helps catch white coat hypertension, where readings are high only in the office.

Suggested rescreening intervals, based on limited evidence:

- Every year for adults 40 and older, and for adults at increased risk, such as Black adults, people with high-normal blood pressure, and people with overweight or obesity.
- Every 3 to 5 years for adults 18 to 39 who are not at increased risk and had a prior normal reading.

The USPSTF screening statement covers detection only. The blood pressure categories and treatment targets come from the 2025 AHA/ACC high blood pressure guideline.
