The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) June 2024 recommendation for adults 65 and older who live in the community and are at increased risk of falling:

- Exercise interventions to prevent falls: recommended (grade B), with a moderate net benefit.
- Multifactorial interventions: clinicians individualize the decision to offer them (grade C), because the net benefit is small.

Effective exercise programs most often included gait, balance and functional training, followed by strength and resistance training, flexibility and endurance work. Some trials used tai chi or group dance. The most common schedule was 2 to 3 sessions a week for about 12 months, delivered as group classes or supervised individual physical therapy.

Multifactorial programs start with an assessment of fall risk factors such as balance, gait, vision, blood pressure on standing, medications, home hazards, thinking and mood, then tailor follow-up to what is found. Vitamin D for fall prevention is reviewed separately and is not part of this recommendation.
