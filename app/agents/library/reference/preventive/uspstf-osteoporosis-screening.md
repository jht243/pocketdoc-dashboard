The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) January 2025 recommendation on screening for osteoporosis (thin, fragile bones) to prevent fractures:

- Women 65 and older: screen (grade B).
- Postmenopausal women younger than 65 who are at increased risk of an osteoporotic fracture, as estimated by a clinical risk assessment tool: screen (grade B).
- Men: evidence is insufficient to weigh the benefits and harms of screening (I statement).

Screening uses a bone mineral density test, usually a DXA scan, with or without a fracture risk assessment tool. The recommendation applies to adults 40 and older without known osteoporosis or a prior fragility fracture, meaning a break from a fall at standing height or less. People already diagnosed or who have had such a fracture are managed under treatment guidelines rather than screening. Keywords: bone density scan, DEXA, DXA, osteoporosis test, bone thinning.
