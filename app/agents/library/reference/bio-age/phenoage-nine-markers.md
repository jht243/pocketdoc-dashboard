The Levine 2018 PhenoAge biological age model uses chronological age plus nine blood markers, all available from three common tests:

- Comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP): albumin, creatinine, fasting glucose, alkaline phosphatase (ALP)
- Complete blood count with differential (CBC with diff): white blood cell count (WBC), lymphocyte percent, mean cell volume (MCV), red cell distribution width (RDW)
- High-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP)

In the published equation, higher glucose, creatinine, CRP, MCV, RDW, alkaline phosphatase and WBC push the estimate older, while higher albumin and higher lymphocyte percent push it younger. RDW carries a relatively large weight in the formula.

The coefficients only work in the units the paper used (for example albumin in g/L, creatinine in micromol/L, glucose in mmol/L, CRP as the log of mg/dL), so US lab values in g/dL, mg/dL and mg/L must be converted before calculating. A standard CBC without the differential lacks lymphocyte percent, and a standard CRP test may not report low values precisely, so a missing marker usually means adding a CBC with differential or an hs-CRP.
