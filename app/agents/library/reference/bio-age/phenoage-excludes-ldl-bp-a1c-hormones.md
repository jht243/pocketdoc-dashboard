The Levine 2018 PhenoAge equation contains only chronological age and nine markers: albumin, creatinine, fasting glucose, hs-CRP, lymphocyte percent, MCV, RDW, alkaline phosphatase and white blood cell count. It does not include LDL cholesterol, total or HDL cholesterol, triglycerides, ApoB, Lp(a), blood pressure, hemoglobin A1c, body weight or BMI, thyroid tests, vitamin D, or any sex hormone such as testosterone or estradiol.

Because these values are not in the formula, changing them has no mathematical effect on a PhenoAge result, even when the change is clinically important. Lowering LDL cholesterol or blood pressure lowers cardiovascular risk, but it will not move biological age.

For that reason Thumbprint shows cholesterol and blood pressure effects through a second published score, Heart Age, built on the AHA PREVENT equations, which do include total cholesterol, HDL and systolic blood pressure. An A1c improvement also does not appear in PhenoAge, although fasting glucose, which is in the formula, often moves with it.
